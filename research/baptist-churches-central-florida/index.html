Research project · Central Florida

Mapping Baptist churches responsibly

A source-tracked register in development. We do not yet have a verified count of Baptist churches in the Orlando area.

A directory listing is evidence that a congregation appeared in that directory, not proof that it is currently open. We will publish totals and change records only after the geographic scope, inclusion rules, and each source snapshot are documented.

What the register will record

Each church record needs a stable name, city, source URL, date checked, denomination or association when explicitly stated, current-status evidence, and review notes. A change log will distinguish a newly discovered listing from a confirmed opening, and a missing listing from a confirmed closure.

Scope and counting rules

The initial scope is Orange and Seminole counties, Florida. One physical congregation is counted once, even when it appears in several directories. Campuses and church plants require separate evidence before they are counted separately. The register cannot represent all Baptists unless sources cover Baptist churches outside any one association.

Sources under review

The Greater Orlando Baptist Association member list includes Church Spasenie in Winter Park. The Florida Baptist Convention church finder is a starting point for one convention, not a complete Baptist census. Its Central Region list is dated January 11, 2021, so entries need fresh checks. The Baptist World Alliance member directory lists associations rather than individual local congregations.

Current result

No geographic total, opening, closure, or net-change figure is published yet. The register schema is available for inspection; its rows are pending source-by-source verification. A count of zero records would describe our work in progress, not the number of churches in the area.

Correction or source?

If you know of an official directory or a church's own announcement, send the URL to hello@ChurchSpasenie.com. We will verify it before adding it to the register.
