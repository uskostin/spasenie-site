Why churches celebrate the Harvest Festival

For the Spasenie Church community, this is a day to thank God for His generosity and blessings.

At a glance: The Harvest Festival is a time to thank God for what He has given. The community gathers for worship and shares a festive lunch afterward.

The church invitation quotes James 1:17: “Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights.” These words set the theme for the gathering: gratitude to God for His generosity and blessings.

Giving thanks together

A busy week can draw attention to what is missing. The Harvest Festival gives the community time to remember God’s gifts and thank Him together.

After the service, the church invites guests to share a festive lunch. You may stay for the meal or leave after worship.

An invitation for guests

You do not need to know anyone at Spasenie Church before attending. The 2026 Harvest Festival service begins at 1:00 PM on September 27 in Winter Park.
