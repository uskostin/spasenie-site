Your first visit to a Russian-speaking church in Orlando

Service time, address, language, and what to expect on your first Sunday at Spasenie Church in Winter Park.

At a glance: Sunday worship begins at 1:00 PM at 701 Formosa Avenue, Winter Park, FL 32789. No registration is required. The service is in Russian and usually lasts about 90 minutes.

You do not need to prepare in a special way. There is no advance registration or dress code. Check the current schedule and allow enough time for the drive.

If this is your first visit, save the complete address in your map app. Winter Park is near Orlando, and travel time varies by neighborhood.

What happens during the service

The service includes singing, preaching, and prayer. A first-time guest may simply take a seat and listen. You do not need to know the songs or join every part of the service.

The congregation shares lunch afterward. Staying is optional. The meal offers a relaxed way to meet people without a formal introduction.

Visiting with children

Children stay with their parents. Stepping out with a young child during the service is fine. We do not promise a separate children’s program unless the church confirms its schedule and format.

Common questions

Do I need to register?

No advance registration is required.

What time does worship begin?

Sunday at 1:00 PM.

May I bring children?

Yes. Children may stay with their parents during the service.

Before you leave

Check the schedule on the official website. Email hello@ChurchSpasenie.com or call +1 407-900-3456 with questions.
