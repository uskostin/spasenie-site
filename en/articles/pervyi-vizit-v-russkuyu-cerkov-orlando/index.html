Your first visit to Spasenie Church: what to expect

Planning to visit a Russian-speaking church near Orlando? Here is a guide to your first Sunday at Spasenie in Winter Park.

At a glance: Sunday worship begins at 1:00 PM at 701 Formosa Avenue, Winter Park, FL 32789. No registration is required. The service is in Russian and usually lasts about 90 minutes.

You do not need to prepare in a special way. There is no advance registration or dress code. Check the current schedule and allow enough time for the drive.

Save the complete address in your map app. Winter Park is near Orlando, and travel time depends on where you start. The address and schedule page links to the church’s Google Maps listing and explains the Thursday meeting.

What happens during the service

The service includes singing, preaching, and prayer. A first-time guest may simply take a seat and listen. You do not need to know the songs or join every part of the service.

The congregation shares lunch afterward. Staying is optional. The meal offers a relaxed way to meet people without a formal introduction.

The language of the service

Worship is held primarily in Russian. If you are bringing English-speaking friends or family, contact the church beforehand to ask what help is available. An English version of the website makes it easier to plan a visit; it does not mean that interpretation is provided during worship.

Visiting with children

Children may stay with their parents. Stepping out with a young child during the service is fine. If a separate children’s program is important for your visit, ask the church about its availability and timing before you travel.

Questions before you leave

Call ahead if you need information about parking, bringing a stroller or building accessibility. You can discuss your particular needs rather than relying on a general guide. Check church news for special gatherings and schedule changes.

You can also read about the congregation on the Spasenie Church homepage. If you have questions after your first visit, use the email or phone below to continue the conversation.

Before you leave

Check the schedule on the official website. Email hello@ChurchSpasenie.com or call +1 407-900-3456 with questions.
