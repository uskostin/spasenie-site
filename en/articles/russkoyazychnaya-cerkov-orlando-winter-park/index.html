Spasenie Church: address and service times in Winter Park

Find our Russian-speaking congregation near Orlando and plan your Sunday or Thursday visit.

At a glance: Spasenie Church meets at 701 Formosa Avenue, Winter Park, FL 32789. Sunday worship begins at 1:00 PM, and the Thursday meeting begins at 7:00 PM.

The address to use for directions

Spasenie Church

If you are searching for a Russian-speaking church in Orlando, use Winter Park in your navigation app: that is the city in our meeting address. The Google Maps listing is named Church Spasenie — Russian Speaking Baptist Church.

Plan your route from your own starting point, whether you are coming from Orlando or elsewhere in Central Florida. Travel times depend on traffic and your location, so check your map app before leaving.

Sunday worship and Thursday Bible study

Sunday worship begins at 1:00 PM, local Florida time. The service includes singing, preaching and prayer and usually lasts about 90 minutes. You can stay for a shared lunch afterward.

Bible study and prayer begin at 7:00 PM on Thursdays. Meetings are held primarily in Russian. If you need help in English, please contact the church before your visit to ask what assistance is available.

Planning a first visit

You do not need to register for a regular Sunday service. Our first-visit guide explains what to expect during worship, visiting with children and staying for lunch. For questions about the entrance, parking or building accessibility, call before leaving so you can get information for your circumstances.

Check church news for special events or schedule changes. To learn about the congregation, visit the Spasenie Church homepage.
