Harvest Festival in Orlando: join us September 27

Spasenie Church invites Russian-speaking residents of Orlando and nearby communities to Sunday worship and a festive lunch.

At a glance: Join us Sunday, September 27, 2026 at 1:00 PM at 701 Formosa Avenue, Winter Park, FL 32789. A festive lunch will follow the service.

Date and timeSeptember 27 at 1:00 PM
Address701 Formosa Avenue
After the serviceFestive lunch

The church flyer describes the Harvest Festival as a time to thank God for His generosity and blessings. It includes James 1:17: “Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights.”

You are welcome even if you have never visited Spasenie Church. The invitation is for the whole family. No advance registration or special clothing is required. The service is held in Russian.

After the service

The congregation will share a festive lunch. Guests may stay for the meal, meet people, and spend time together. Staying for lunch is optional.
